# SEO Audit Summary — toster.co
Date: 2026-05-05
Phase: 1 (Competitive intelligence + keyword mapping)

---

## 1. Main competitor patterns found

### The Syrve/Restasystem template machine
Syrve (formerly iiko, sold via Restasystem) is the most aggressive SEO actor across UA, PL, CS, and DE. They run near-identical translated landing pages per locale: same H2 structure, same ~2,200 words, same FAQ (8 questions), and a 3-column feature table. Their CRM pages position Syrve as a loyalty/guest management tool, not a delivery operations platform. All their pages are missing meta descriptions. Zero JSON-LD schema.

### Aggregators dominate EN
In English, Capterra, GetApp, G2, and SoftwareAdvice own the category pages. They are nearly impossible to outrank directly. The actual product pages ranking (PepperCloud, Glide Apps) are horizontal tools, not vertical SaaS — very weak content authority for the specific use case.

### OneBox OS (UA) — keyword-stuffed but effective
OneBox ranks in Ukraine with 17 H2s all using the exact keyword. This is a generic business platform, not a restaurant-specific tool. Their page has a pricing table (strong conversion element) and a FAQ section. Their weakness: complexity, requiring implementation partners.

### FoodSoul — free but invisible
FoodSoul ranks via domain authority despite having a JavaScript SPA with no crawlable content. "Free" is their hook. No information about features, chain support, or operations.

### GoPOS (PL) — operations-focused but siloed
GoPOS GoDelivery covers courier tracking and aggregator integrations better than any competitor in Polish. But it has no loyalty/CRM features on the delivery page. It is a module add-on, not an all-in-one platform.

### WinOrder (DE) — legacy desktop incumbent
WinOrder is the dominant delivery management tool in Germany but is a legacy Windows desktop application. No cloud, no mobile, no chain management. Strong incumbent that is vulnerable to a modern cloud challenger.

### No one owns the "chain + delivery + CRM" intersection
Across all 7 locales, not a single competitor occupies the specific position of: multi-location restaurant chain + integrated delivery dispatch + customer loyalty CRM + kitchen display + courier tracking — all in one cloud product. This is toster.co's core differentiator and the primary SEO opportunity.

## 2. Weak spots in top results (where toster.co can win)

1. **Missing meta descriptions** — all top competitor pages lack meta descriptions. Writing tight, benefit-first meta descriptions is a zero-effort quick win for click-through rate.

2. **No JSON-LD schema** — none of the top-3 competitors in any locale use SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, or Review schema. Implementing these could win Featured Snippets and rich results.

3. **JavaScript SPA pages rank** (FoodSoul UA) — a text-rich HTML page from toster.co will outrank a content-empty SPA in weeks.

4. **Horizontal CRMs filling vertical gaps** (EN) — PepperCloud and Glide rank for "food delivery CRM software" despite not being food-delivery-native. A genuinely vertical product page with specific use-case content will outrank them.

5. **Translation-only localization** — Syrve copies the same page to all locales. Pages with genuine local market insight (local competitors, local pricing context, local case studies) will rank higher and convert better.

6. **No multi-location content** — the chain/network management angle is almost completely absent from all competitor pages. This is toster.co's primary strength and a gap in every locale.

7. **FAQ sections absent from ~50% of top results** — implementing FAQPage schema with 8–10 real questions wins Position 0 (Featured Snippet) in low-competition locales.

8. **Word count gap** — competitors average 2,200–2,500 words. Toster.co pages at 3,500–4,500 words with better structure will outperform on topical authority.

9. **No named case studies** — no competitor page features named restaurant clients with measurable results. Even one case study ("Restaurant chain X reduced courier wait time by 23%") creates a content moat.

10. **"Alternative" pages missing in local languages** — there are no Polish, Czech, or German-language "alternative to [X]" pages for most competitors. These conversion-intent pages can be built with low competition.

## 3. Recommended content structure

### Primary landing pages (one per locale, per core keyword)
- **Target word count**: 3,500–4,500 words
- **Must-have H2s** (in this order):
  1. What is [product category] software? (defines category, captures informational intent)
  2. Key features: [list of 6–8 features with brief descriptions]
  3. Who is it for? (single location vs chain — segment clearly)
  4. How [toster.co] handles delivery dispatch (courier tracking, GPS, Telegram bot)
  5. Loyalty program and customer CRM
  6. Kitchen display and order flow
  7. Managing multiple locations from one dashboard
  8. Integrations (aggregators, payment, maps)
  9. Pricing (be transparent — this differentiates from Syrve consultation model)
  10. [Competitor] vs toster.co comparison table
  11. Customer success story / case study
  12. FAQ (8–10 questions using FAQPage schema)
  13. Get started / CTA

### Comparison pages (one per major competitor per locale)
- **Target word count**: 2,500–3,000 words
- Must-have sections: feature comparison table, pricing comparison, who should switch and why
- Target: "альтернатива Poster POS", "Syrve alternative", "WinOrder Alternative", etc.

### Blog / D-tier content
- **Target word count**: 1,500–2,500 words per post
- Topics: automation guides, chain management how-tos, KDS benefits, loyalty program setup
- Include internal links to product pages
- 1–2 posts per locale per month in Year 1

## 4. Top 10 quick wins (low competition, high potential)

1. **CRM для мережі ресторанів (uk)** — Zero competition. Multi-location + delivery + CRM combined keyword. Toster.co owns this use case. Write a 3,500-word page, implement FAQPage schema, get a Featured Snippet.

2. **FoodSoul SPA displacement (uk)** — FoodSoul ranks with a JavaScript SPA with no text. Any text-rich HTML page targeting "CRM для доставки їжі" outranks them within 4–8 weeks.

3. **Software Lieferdienst Management Kette (de)** — No German-language page covers multi-location delivery chain management. Competition from SIDES and WinOrder is beatable with genuinely better content.

4. **CRM pro rozvoz jídla (cs)** — Czech market is very thin on SaaS competition. Syrve's translated page is the main competitor. A 3,000+ word Czech-native page with local competitor mentions (Storyous, Dotykačka) can reach position 1 within 3 months.

5. **Alternativa Storyous / alternativa Dotykačka (cs)** — These comparison keywords have near-zero competition and high buyer intent. Czech restaurant owners searching for Storyous alternatives are ready to buy. One 2,500-word comparison page per competitor.

6. **Bot Telegram para pedidos restaurante (es/ru/uk)** — No competitor ranks for Telegram bot + restaurant delivery. Toster.co's Telegram bots (7 bots across the order flow) are a genuine differentiator. A blog post in each of these locales targets a completely uncontested keyword with high relevance to toster.co's actual feature.

7. **Meta description recovery (all locales)** — Every single top competitor page lacks a meta description. Writing a 155-character, benefit-first meta description for every toster.co page immediately improves CTR vs competitors in SERP.

8. **FAQPage JSON-LD schema (all locales)** — Zero competitors use FAQ schema. Implementing FAQPage JSON-LD on every product page and blog post captures Featured Snippet positions for question-based queries ("jak wybrać CRM", "co jest KDS", etc.).

9. **SoftwareApplication schema (all locales)** — Adding SoftwareApplication, AggregateRating, and Offer schema makes toster.co eligible for rich results (stars, price, platform) — visible advantages none of the competitors have.

10. **"Reducir comisiones Glovo Uber Eats restaurante (es)"** — High-intent, zero direct competition. Restaurant owners searching how to reduce aggregator commissions (15–30% per order) are the exact buyers for toster.co's own ordering system. A detailed blog post + CTA converts at high rates.
