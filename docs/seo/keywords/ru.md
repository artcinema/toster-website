# Keywords: ru (Russian)

Date: 2026-05-05

## Query: "CRM для доставки еды автоматизация ресторана Россия"
Top domains appearing: academy.chibbis.ru, restocrm.com, crmindex.ru, ecopack-group.ru, emchd.ru, tillypad.ru, datakrat.ru, traktir.ru
Notable patterns: RestoCRM is a dedicated Russian-market food delivery CRM with iiko/r_keeper sync. Frontpad, Quick Resto, SBIS Presto are Russian-native SaaS alternatives. Chibbis is a food delivery aggregator with its own CRM academy. R-Keeper Delivery (Datakrat) targets complex chain operations. Tillypad is a mid-market player. Russian market isolated from EU/UA vendors post-2022.

## Query: "альтернатива iiko система автоматизации ресторана 2026"
Top domains appearing: restik.com, picktech.ru, joinposter.com, pos-life.ru, posbazar.ru, komiinform.ru, livebusiness.ru, multi-bit.com, aqba.ru
Notable patterns: iiko is the dominant Russian restaurant automation system (now rebranded Syrve internationally). R-Keeper is iiko's main competitor. Restik is the leading cloud alternative for SMB. Poster POS is present (Ukrainian origin, still used in RU). SBIS Presto is a government-registry-certified Russian software. Comparison portals (livebusiness.ru, picktech.ru) rank well.

## Query: "система автоматизации доставки управление сетью ресторанов"
Top domains appearing: restocrm.com, emchd.ru, crmindex.ru, restik.com
Notable patterns: No single platform dominates the "network/chain" + "delivery" intersection in Russian. Most solutions focus on single-location automation. Chain management content is sparse.

## Query: "управление сетью ресторанов программа"
Top domains appearing: iiko.ru, r-keeper.ru, restocrm.com, quickresto.ru
Notable patterns: iiko and R-Keeper own the chain management space in Russia. Quick Resto is the cloud challenger. Very limited modern alternatives with multi-location delivery features. Opportunity for toster.co content targeting CIS diaspora (UA/PL communities with Russian-language search habits).
