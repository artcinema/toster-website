## Query: "alternatywa Poster POS Polska system restauracja"
Top domains appearing: posbistro.com, upmenu.com, restaumatic.com, ipos.pl, papu.io, gopos.pl, wojciechmatula.com, wingin.pl
Notable patterns: POSbistro is the leading Polish-developed POS. Dotykačka (Czech origin) is used in Poland. GoPOS is the strongest local alternative. Restaumatic focuses on online ordering. PAPU.io is a delivery-specific system (3,000 couriers/day). No dominant "alternatives" hub in Polish.

## Query: "system zarządzania dostawą jedzenia oprogramowanie Polska"
Top domains appearing: capterra.com, papu.io, gopos.pl, escsa.pl, delivety.com, abspos.pl, fodimi.pl
Notable patterns: PAPU.io is the clear market leader for delivery management (350 orders/minute, 3,000 couriers). GoPOS GoDelivery is a modular add-on. ABS POS integrates Uber Eats, Glovo, Wolt, Pyszne.pl. Delivety is an EU-wide delivery management platform. Aggregator integrations are key selling point in Poland.
