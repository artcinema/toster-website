## Query: "CRM para reparto de comida software gestión restaurantes España"
Top domains appearing: ahora.es, last.app, salesforce.com/es, larksuite.com, delivety.com, apperstreet.com, pipedrive.com, makro.es, chetu.com, arbentia.com
Notable patterns: Apperstreet claims to be "el primer CRM especializado para restaurantes de comida a domicilio" (first specialized delivery CRM for restaurants). Last.app is a strong Spanish-market all-in-one POS+delivery platform. Salesforce and Pipedrive are horizontal CRMs adapted for restaurants. Delivety is a cross-EU delivery management tool. Ahora.es is a Spanish ERP/CRM vendor. Makro has a content marketing guide. No dominant vertical SaaS for multi-location delivery chains in Spain.

## Query: "software gestión restaurantes con delivery España"
Top domains appearing: last.app, ahora.es, delivety.com, upmenu.com, gofrugal.com
Notable patterns: Last.app is the most modern Spanish-language all-in-one. UpMenu has Spanish content. GoFrugal targets chains. Very little Spanish-language content for multi-chain delivery operations specifically.

## Query: "alternativa Toast POS España plataforma delivery restaurante"
Top domains appearing: peblla.com, qamarero.com, capterra.com, monouso.es, umappi.com, last.app, idoo.restaurant
Notable patterns: Toast POS is US-centric and not directly available in Spain. Qamarero is the leading Spanish QR ordering platform. UMAPPI is a "white-label" commission-free delivery alternative to aggregators. Idoo.restaurant is a Spanish all-in-one POS. Peblla is an international POS review site.

## Query: "plataforma gestión delivery comida restaurante sistema"
Top domains appearing: last.app, delivety.com, apperstreet.com, umappi.com, idoo.restaurant
Notable patterns: Last.app and Delivety dominate. No chain-management-first platform visible in Spanish results. Strong opportunity: no one covers multi-location chain + delivery + CRM + loyalty + courier tracking in Spanish.
